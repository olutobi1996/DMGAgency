Dominance Sports Group was built on a simple belief: an athlete's career is bigger than any single contract. We work across six sports because talent doesn't stay in one lane — and neither should representation.

From a rookie's first professional deal to a veteran's transition into ownership, media or coaching, our teams stay close to the people we represent and the families behind them. We negotiate hard, plan further ahead, and treat every relationship as a decade-long commitment, not a single transaction.
